# Fictional demonstration policies
These policies are fictional examples for testing. They are not company-approved policies.

## Annual leave
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year. Request annual leave through the HR portal at least five working days in advance. Your manager must approve the request before leave begins.

## Sick leave
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year. Notify your manager before your shift where possible. For sick leave lasting more than three consecutive working days, submit a medical certificate to HR.

## Remote work
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Eligible employees may work remotely from home up to two days per week with manager approval. Submit a remote work request in the HR portal. Certain roles require office attendance.

## Health benefits
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Health insurance benefits begin on the first day of employment. Employees can request medical coverage enrollment information from HR. Dependent coverage is subject to the insurer’s plan terms.

## Travel expenses
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Submit travel expense reimbursement claims within 30 days of travel. Attach itemized receipts and manager approval. Personal purchases are not reimbursable expenses.

## Workplace conduct
DEMO POLICY ONLY. Report workplace harassment or discrimination confidentially to the HR team. Retaliation for raising a good-faith complaint is prohibited. HR will explain the grievance review process.

## Onboarding and probation
DEMO POLICY ONLY. New employees complete onboarding orientation during their first week. The probation period is 90 days. HR and the manager coordinate the probation review.
