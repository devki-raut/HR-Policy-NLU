Privacy Policy

EmployeeAssist · Last updated September 29, 2026

Summary: EmployeeAssist processes questions to find answers and excerpts from your organization’s HR policy library. It does not access employee records or perform HR transactions.

1. Information processed

When you use the web application or Microsoft Teams bot, the service may process:

  • Your policy question and the conversation identifier needed to maintain the chat session.
  • For Teams messages, the Microsoft Teams or Entra user identifier supplied with the activity.
  • An optional API key submitted by the web page when API protection is enabled. The page does not save that key in browser storage.
  • Technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, such as timestamps, request status, and application errors.

2. How information is used

The service uses this information to classify the question with Rasa, match it against reviewed FAQ entries, retrieve relevant policy chunks, maintain short conversation context, and return an answer with source references.

3. HR policy documents

Authorized administrators can upload HR policy PDFs. The service extracts text, creates searchable chunks, and stores document metadata and derived embeddings. Answers may include exact excerpts and links to the underlying policy PDF. Access to the application and its PDF links should be limited according to your organization’s access rules.

4. Automated processing

The current application uses a Rasa intent classifier and a sentence-transformer embedding model for FAQ matching. These components select relevant reviewed content; they do not make employment decisions. Your deployment administrator may configure additional model services, in which case the organization is responsible for reviewing their data-handling terms.

5. Storage and retention

  • Conversation state is maintained for the Rasa session configured by the service.
  • Server and container logs may retain operational events according to the deployment administrator’s logging and retention configuration.
  • Uploaded policies, extracted text, FAQ mappings, and embeddings remain until an authorized administrator replaces or removes them.
  • The browser creates a random conversation identifier for the current page session and does not intentionally store chat history in local storage.

6. Sharing and service providers

The service communicates with Microsoft Teams and Azure Bot Service when deployed as a Teams bot. Policy questions are sent to the Rasa service configured by the application. The project does not sell personal information or use policy questions for advertising.

7. Security and appropriate use

The application supports an optional API key, container isolation, and organization-managed Microsoft credentials. No system is completely secure. Do not enter passwords, financial account details, medical records, or other sensitive personal information unless your organization has explicitly approved that use.

8. Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. Contact your HR team or system administrator to ask about access, correction, deletion, retention, or the policies configured for your organization.

9. Changes

This policy may change as the application or its deployment changes. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are published.
