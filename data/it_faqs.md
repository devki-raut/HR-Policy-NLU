# IT FAQs from the incident report

Historical resolutions are investigation leads, not confirmed diagnoses. Empty solutions and closure-only notes do not establish a technical fix.

## My office Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting. What should I check?

Check whether the problem affects other devices and whether a wired connection remains stable. Reconnect to the approved Wi-Fi network and note the location, time and frequency of the disconnections. If several users are affected, IT should check the access point and network link.

## I’m connected to Wi-Fi, but websites won’t load. What can I try?

Try another website and check whether another device on the same Wi-Fi can browse. If only your device is affected, disconnect and reconnect to the approved Wi-Fi network. If multiple devices are affected, note the location, start time and browser error for IT.

## The office internet is very slow today. How can I troubleshoot it?

Check whether the slowdown affects one application or all websites, and compare Wi-Fi with a wired connection if available. Note when it occurs and whether colleagues are affected. IT can then check link quality, utilisation and provider faults.

## My laptop cannot connect to the company Wi-Fi. What should I do?

Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled and select the approved company network. Forget and reconnect to the network only if permitted, then compare with another device. Record any authentication error and send it to IT without sharing the Wi-Fi password.

## The VPN disconnects every few minutes. What could be causing this?

Check whether ordinary websites remain stable when the VPN disconnects. Retry the approved VPN client once and compare another approved network if available. Note the exact error, time and whether the device or network recently changed so IT can check the connection and registration.

## Outlook says my account is locked. What should I do?

Try signing in through Outlook on the web and record the exact lock message. Do not repeatedly retry the password or share credentials. IT must verify the account lock and authentication status.

## I can receive emails but cannot send them. What should I check?

Check whether Outlook is offline and whether messages are accumulating in the Outbox. Look for a large attachment or sending error, then test Outlook on the web. Avoid repeatedly sending duplicate copies.

## Outlook keeps asking me to sign in. How can I fix this?

Check whether Outlook on the web accepts your work account and whether other Microsoft 365 services work. Record the exact prompt and whether it affects desktop, web or mobile. Do not share your password or MFA code with IT.

## My emails are stuck in the Outbox. What should I check?

Check that Outlook is online and your internet connection works. Look for a large attachment or a message showing an error. Try Outlook on the web and avoid sending duplicate copies.

## I cannot open my work mailbox. What can I try?

Check your connection and try Outlook on the web. Note whether the mailbox fails on web, desktop and mobile, and capture the exact error. Mailbox provisioning or access changes require IT review.

## My laptop won’t start after an update. What should I do?

Disconnect nonessential accessories, connect the approved charger and check for lights or error messages. Try one normal restart. Record what appears on screen; further recovery or repair should be handled by IT.

## My laptop screen is black, but the power light is on. How can I troubleshoot it?

Check the screen brightness and disconnect an external display or dock if one is attached. Try the approved display shortcut or one normal restart. Note any fan, keyboard or startup sounds for IT.

## My laptop battery is not charging. What should I check?

Check that the approved charger is firmly connected to the laptop and a working outlet. Inspect it for visible damage and note whether Windows detects the charger. Do not use a damaged charger; ask IT to inspect it.

## My laptop freezes whenever I open Excel. What can I try?

Check whether Excel freezes with every workbook or only one file. Try opening Excel without that file and note any error or problematic add-in. Save your work and ask IT to review repeated crashes.

## My keyboard has stopped working. How can I troubleshoot it?

Reconnect an external keyboard or try another approved USB port. For a laptop keyboard, restart once and check whether the issue affects all keys. Report liquid damage, physical damage or persistent failure to IT.

## How do I request access to the project shared drive?

Provide the shared-drive or folder name, required permission level, business reason and approving owner. IT must review and grant access; access to another employee’s data should not be assumed from previous requests.

## I get “Access denied” when opening the team folder. What should I check?

Confirm you are signed in with the correct work account and copy the exact folder path and error. Check whether colleagues with the same role can open it. The folder owner or IT must review your permissions.

## What is the process for getting access to the reporting application?

Provide the application name, required role, business purpose and manager or application-owner approval. IT or the application owner must provision the access.

## I cannot log in to the company portal. What can I try?

Confirm you are using the approved portal and correct work account, then test another work service. Record the exact sign-in error without sharing your password or MFA code. IT can check account status and permissions.

## My permissions to the shared workspace were removed. Who can restore them?

The workspace owner or IT must verify and restore the permissions. Provide the workspace name, access level previously held, when access stopped and any approval or error message.

## Teams calls have no audio through my headset. What should I check?

In Teams device settings, select the correct headset for speaker and microphone and run a test call. Reconnect the headset and check whether it works in another application.

## Other people cannot hear me in Teams meetings. How can I troubleshoot my microphone?

Confirm you are not muted, select the correct microphone in Teams and run a test call. Check the operating-system microphone permission and whether another application is using the microphone.

## My camera is not working in Teams. What can I try?

Select the correct camera in Teams, close other applications using it and check camera permissions. Try a Teams test call and note whether the camera works in another approved application.

## Teams closes whenever I join a meeting. How can I troubleshoot this?

Check whether Teams is updated, restart it and try another meeting or the web version. Note whether the crash occurs in every meeting and capture any error for IT.

## I cannot share my screen in Teams. What should I check?

Confirm screen-sharing permission is enabled for Teams and try sharing a single window. Check whether the meeting organizer restricts sharing. Record any error and whether the issue occurs in the desktop and web clients.

## Citrix says access is denied. What should I check?

Check that ordinary websites work and retry the approved Citrix client once. Record the complete error and whether you recently changed device or network. IT should verify your entitlement, device registration and security checks; do not bypass the restriction.

## Citrix stopped working after I changed my laptop. What should I do?

Provide your user ID, device MAC address and an error screenshot through the approved support channel. This historical fix is not a confirmed diagnosis of the current issue.

## My VPN will not connect even though normal websites work. What should I check?

## Citrix login fails, but IT cannot find any login logs. What should be checked?
